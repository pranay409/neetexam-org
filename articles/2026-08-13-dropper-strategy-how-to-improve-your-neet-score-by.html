Dropper Strategy: Boost Your NEET Score by 100+ Marks

A practical roadmap for droppers to achieve their medical dreams

Taking a drop year after NEET is a brave decision that shows commitment to your goals. But here's the truth: most droppers struggle because they repeat the same preparation mistakes instead of fundamentally changing their approach. If you're seeing scores in the 550-600 range and aiming for 650+, you need a strategic overhaul, not just more study hours.

Understand Your Bottleneck

Before diving into study plans, spend the first week analyzing exactly where you lost marks in your previous attempt. Were you weak in specific subjects like Botany or Inorganic Chemistry? Did you struggle with time management? Were careless mistakes costing you 15-20 marks? Or did you simply not understand concepts deeply enough?

Most droppers waste months studying broad concepts when they should be laser-focused on their weak areas. Get your previous year's answer key, mark every question you got wrong, and categorize them. This analysis becomes your roadmap for the entire year.

Action item: Create a spreadsheet of your mistakes. Include the topic, difficulty level (easy/medium/hard), and whether it was a conceptual gap or careless error. This single document will guide your entire strategy.
