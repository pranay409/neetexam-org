Understanding AIQ vs State Quota Seats

After your NEET examination, the actual journey of securing a medical seat begins with counselling. This is where most aspirants feel confused about their options. The difference between All India Quota (AIQ) and state quota seats can significantly impact which medical college you end up joining. Let me walk you through this process in a way that makes sense.

What is All India Quota (AIQ)?

All India Quota comprises 15% of the total MBBS and BDS seats available across India. These seats are allocated based on your all-India ranking in NEET, regardless of which state you're from. If you secure an AIQ seat, you can join a medical college in any state in the country.

The major advantage here is flexibility. You're competing on a national level, and your rank determines your seat. There are no domicile restrictions, meaning a student from Kerala can get a seat in Delhi, and vice versa. This is governed by the Medical Counselling Committee (MCC) and follows a transparent online counselling process.
