The Weekly Assessment Framework

Every week in the AIM720 batch, mentors conduct structured assessments that go beyond traditional tests. Progress tracking in the AIM720 program works through several interconnected mechanisms. First, there's the live class participation tracking. During the 2-way live classes, your mentor observes how you're engaging with problems, which questions you're asking, and where confusion arises. Second, there's the structured assignment analysis. Every assignment you submit gets reviewed not just for correctness but for methodology. Your mentor looks at how you're attempting problems, whether you're using efficient approaches, and if you're making conceptual errors or calculation mistakes. The Weekly Mentor-Student Conversation

What makes the Padhle AIM720 approach different from impersonal coaching centers is the weekly one-on-one discussion with your personal mentor. This isn't a cursory check-in. It's a structured conversation where your mentor reviews your progress data and creates an action plan with you for the next week.

Key Point: Your mentor isn't just telling you what to study. They're analyzing your learning velocity, identifying your peak hours of productivity, understanding your weak topics, and strategically planning how to address these before the actual exam.

During these conversations, mentors often uncover patterns you yourself might miss. Armed with this data, they can suggest optimal study intervals, specific revision strategies, or even confidence-building activities.

Actionable Ways Your Progress is Tracked

1. Concept Mastery Assessment

Each week, mentors evaluate whether you've genuinely understood the concepts taught in the previous week. This goes beyond memorization. They ask you to apply concepts in different contexts, solve problems from multiple angles, and explain your reasoning. If you can do all three, your mentor marks that concept as mastered and moves forward. If not, they create a targeted revision plan.

2. Problem-Solving Speed and Accuracy

NEET requires not just accuracy but speed. Your mentor tracks both metrics separately. If you're accurate but slow, they'll suggest different problem-solving strategies. If you're fast but making errors, they'll focus on systematic approaches. This granular tracking ensures you develop the exact skill set required for exam day.

3. Topic-Wise Strength Mapping

By the end of each week, your mentor has a clear picture of which topics you're strongest in and which need more attention. This data is visualized in simple formats that you can understand at a glance, making it easy to adjust your study plan immediately.

4. Consistency and Dedication Metrics

Your mentor also tracks your consistency. Are you completing assignments on time? Are you showing up prepared for live classes? Are you maintaining steady progress or having erratic spikes and dips? Consistency is often more important than brilliance in NEET preparation, and good mentors know this.

Practical Tip: Keep a simple log of what you studied each day and how you felt about different topics. Share this with your mentor during your weekly conversation. This transparency helps them give you better guidance.
